# Ninh Kieu Pharma Pharmacy Management System Specification

Ninh Kieu Pharma operates in Ninh Kieu District, Can Tho City, providing prescription and over-the-counter medications, dietary supplements, and medical supplies. The pharmacy's daily operations include managing staff, registering and scheduling shifts, recording attendance, managing products and suppliers, receiving goods, tracking inventory, consulting with customers at the counter, issuing sales invoices, and consolidating revenue. Its workforce consists of administrators and staff. Currently, work schedules and some employee information are tracked using spreadsheets. The pharmacy also uses basic invoicing software to record medication names, calculate totals, and print invoices, but this software does not cover staff, orders, or inventory management and provides limited reporting.

As the number of staff and transactions grows, manually reconciling employee availability, assigned shifts, and attendance takes considerable time and can lead to scheduling conflicts, understaffing, or update errors. Product, category, supplier, receiving, and inventory information is not managed centrally, requiring staff to consult multiple sources when making sales or checking stock. The lack of a consistent process for stocktaking, adjustments, and inventory movement history delays the detection of discrepancies, damaged goods, and expired products. Fragmented customer profiles and allergy information make it difficult to retrieve information when customers return and to control access to sensitive details. Promotions, post-transaction loyalty point accrual, and point redemption for discounts also need to be managed consistently. In addition, consolidating shift counts, actual working hours, inventory status, order volumes, and revenue requires considerable effort.

The project involves developing the **“Ninh Kieu Pharma Pharmacy Management System”** using the Java Desktop platform to support the pharmacy's in-store operations. The system includes 19 functions: **(1)** managing system accounts and access roles; **(2)** managing employee availability; **(3)** managing store work schedules; **(4)** managing attendance; **(5)** reporting shift counts, actual working hours, and shift coverage rates; **(6)** reporting schedule adherence and punctuality; **(7)** managing product categories; **(8)** managing products; **(9)** managing suppliers; **(10)** reporting category trends; **(11)** reporting supplier coverage and share; **(12)** managing goods receiving; **(13)** managing warehouse locations; **(14)** managing stocktakes and inventory adjustments; **(15)** reporting inventory movements, damaged goods, and expired products; **(16)** managing customers, allergies, and loyalty points; **(17)** managing sales transactions; **(18)** managing promotions and loyalty settings; and **(19)** reporting order volumes and revenue.
